# Product Principles

## 1. Today first
The home screen answers: What do I need to do today?

## 2. Group and PT are different
Group Fitness is class-oriented and lightweight.
Personal Training is individual and longitudinal.

## 3. One person, multiple relationships
A person can attend group classes and also be a PT client.

## 4. Capture what changes decisions
Do not create documentation for documentation's sake.

## 5. Method before automation
Encode the trainer's methodology before adding automated recommendations.

## 6. Human decision remains visible
When a program or session changes, record the reason and evidence.

## 7. Fast during coaching
Session interfaces must be usable while the trainer is moving and coaching.

## 8. History matters
A client's journey should remain understandable months or years later.

## 9. Client experience is a separate layer
The trainer workspace and eventual client-facing experience should share the underlying data model without becoming the same UI.

## 10. Build for the real business
Track group fitness workload and personal training workload separately, with business metrics later.
